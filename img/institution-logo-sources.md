# Institution logos

- `pku.png`: [Peking University seal](https://www.pku.edu.cn/pku_logo_red.png), downloaded from the university's official website.
- `hust.png`: [Huazhong University of Science and Technology logo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Huazhong_University_of_Science_and_Technology_logo.png), downloaded from Wikimedia.
- `nctu.png`: [National Chiao Tung University emblem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:NCTU_emblem.svg), downloaded as a PNG thumbnail from Wikimedia. Uses the institution's emblem from the exchange period, before the 2021 merger.
- `microsoft.png`: square symbol cropped from the [Microsoft header logo](https://uhf.microsoft.com/images/microsoft/RE1Mu3b.png) used on the Microsoft Research website.
- `jd.png`: [JD.com favicon](https://storage.360buyimg.com/retail-mall/mall-common-component/favicon.ico), converted to PNG without changing the artwork.
- `bjtu.png` and `tsinghua.png`: existing repository assets, reused without modification.

These marks identify the institutions in the education and research experience section; ownership remains with the respective institutions.
